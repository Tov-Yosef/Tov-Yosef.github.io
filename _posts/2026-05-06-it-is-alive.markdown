I've had many elements of this project alive in my mind for years. I like the idea of world building and now I'm doing it in a public facing manner in a way that is really for me but hopefully it will carry meaning for you as well. While the world building has already got some very radical language the ideas I think behind it might be slightly more moderate. The ferver is not, however, I am quite passionate about the things I find to be true. I respect passion and envy it when it escapes me.

I'm glad that I am in this place working on a real container for these ideas. I'm an artist at heart. Within all things I do there are elements of flair and peculiarity that I see as the basis for art. I can have a hard time telling art apart from prose or reality when I find that flair and pecularity.

Let me take a moment to restart here. The world building here is just idealistic ways from my personal perspective of how things should work. Whether that be political, social, religious, or any other aspect that finds it's way into the world building. Ultimately and sadly this is a kind of fiction, like most fiction it is bound to contain many truths. More than truth it will reveal about me things that I imagine I don't even yet know. This is a very personal project and will have many many sub projects as it unfolds. I don't know that there is a real aim or finish line, it will live until it has no life left to carry it.

Thank you for joining me here in this way, whatever way it is that has led you here.
